HR outlet allocation

This is the financial allocation layer. It is separate from the daily rescue roster.

Monthly employee cost allocation

break-even source

Allocate each employee's monthly CTC across one or more outlets. The total must equal 100%. These percentages feed outlet break-even and normal outlet costing. A rescue movement in the roster does not change this allocation.

Allocation rules

Example: TM2 may be 100% GP for monthly costing. If TM2 is sent to RSP for one emergency day, the Roster records that operational move, but TM2's monthly HR cost remains allocated to GP for break-even.

If a manager genuinely serves two outlets as a normal arrangement, use 60% RSP + 40% GP (or the appropriate agreed percentages). Do not use daily rescue movements for accounting allocation.
