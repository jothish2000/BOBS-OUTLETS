Monthly employee cost allocation

break-even source

Each employee's verified monthly CTC is fetched from Google Staff Master. Allocate that CTC across one or more outlets. The total must equal 100%. Daily rescue roster movements never change this monthly allocation.

Allocation rules

Example: an employee may be 100% RSP for monthly costing. If temporarily sent to another outlet for one day, that operational movement does not change the monthly allocation.

If an employee normally serves two outlets, use the agreed split such as 60% RSP + 40% GP.
