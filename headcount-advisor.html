Headcount & CTC Capacity Advisor

Determine how many employees an outlet's economics can accommodate from CTC, then calculate the extra sale required when staffing exceeds that capacity.

Planning assumptions

BOBS default
Average employee monthly CTC (₹)
Operating days / month
Minimum operating surplus / employee / month (₹)

Core logic: the advisor is staff-expense driven. It compares outlet gross contribution available for staff with employee CTC. It does not use a sales-per-employee rule.

Excess-staff test: if actual staff exceeds CTC-supported headcount, the advisor calculates the additional monthly contribution needed and converts it into additional daily sales using the outlet gross-margin percentage.

Outlet-by-outlet calculation

0 outlets

How the number is arrived at

1. Monthly employee cost = CTC. 2. Outlet monthly gross contribution = daily gross profit × operating days. 3. CTC capacity = floor(gross contribution available for staff ÷ CTC). 4. If actual staff is above capacity, excess staff cost is identified. 5. Required additional sale = excess staff cost ÷ gross-margin %.
