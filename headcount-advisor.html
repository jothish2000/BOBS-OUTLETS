Headcount & CTC Capacity Advisor

Determine how many employees an outlet's economics can accommodate from CTC, then calculate the extra sale required when staffing exceeds that capacity.

Core logic: the advisor is now staff-expense driven. It compares the outlet's available gross contribution / operating surplus with employee CTC. It does not decide headcount from a sales-per-employee rule.

Excess-staff test: if actual staff exceeds the CTC-supported headcount, the advisor calculates the additional monthly contribution needed from that excess employee and converts it into the additional daily sale required using the outlet's gross-margin percentage.

How the number is arrived at

1. Monthly employee cost = CTC. 2. Outlet monthly gross contribution = daily gross profit × working days. 3. CTC capacity = floor(gross contribution available for staff ÷ CTC). 4. If actual staff is above that capacity, excess staff cost is identified. 5. Required additional sale = excess staff cost ÷ gross-margin %. This makes the staffing recommendation directly relevant to the project's staff-expense and break-even model.
