What is this page for? What does “publish” mean here?

It is for the person setting up the shared BOBS recipe library for the first time. It saves three things: the prepared market references, BOBS quantity adjustments (calibration), and BOBS standard recipes.

“Publish” means saving this prepared information into BOBS’s Google Sheets. It does not mean posting recipes publicly on the internet or doing a new Google search.

How do I correct a shared BOBS standard later?

Ask the person maintaining BOBS to compare the proposed correction with the saved version, back it up, and save an approved revision. This page cannot make that revision. Editing your outlet recipe in the Recipe cost editor is a separate action.

The old Recipe Master is kept as historical backup. It is not used as the operational fallback recipe.
