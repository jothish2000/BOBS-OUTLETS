Architecture

analytics first

BOBS is the management analytics and decision engine. Your future purchased cloud-compatible software remains the actual transaction/accounting system. Later, its data can feed BOBS through an integration layer. M1 and M2 only change the analytical source/calculation; they never change accounting principles.

Asset Intelligence

new

BOBS treats depreciation as an economic/replacement cost alongside electricity, maintenance and other running costs. Asset Economics answers how much revenue an asset must generate to cover its full economic burden and how many product units are required to recover the investment.

Forecast Intelligence

new

BOBS forecasts both demand and economics: normal sales, festival/event uplift, Avani marriage-season demand, sudden bulk orders, capacity constraints, cost inflation, margin pressure and the additional sales/productivity required to remain sustainable. The engine is planning intelligence, not the future accounting transaction system.

Google Sheets

source of truth

Google Sheets / Data Vault remains the permanent BOBS source of truth. The existing Apps Script deployment is reused; modules save and verify their records against Google rather than treating browser localStorage as the permanent database.
