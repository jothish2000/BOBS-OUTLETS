Operating model

BOBS is designed to start with outlet sales, then recommend headcount, then use the staff/THP model to recruit. Each employee has a home outlet for normal cost ownership, while the daily roster can temporarily move the employee to another outlet for rescue coverage. Outlet costing reads both fixed/variable expenses and rostered staff cost.

Google Sheets remains the central reporting copy once its Apps Script backend is updated for the BOBS outlet/staff costing schema.
