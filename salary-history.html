Each Save stores the complete salary package, including additions, deductions, perks and excluded components. Before replacing a saved package, BOBS verifies a backup in Google Sheets. This page reads those versions without changing them.

History records the salary proposal. Planned positions require the outlet plan to be saved before they affect expenses; linked employees require a successful Staff Master update. A saved proposal alone does not prove it was applied.
