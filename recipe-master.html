Reading saved history from Google Sheets…

Why is there no Save button? These are older records kept for reference. Editing them here could change the history, so this page only reads Google Sheets.

For your current recipe: return to your open Item Editor or Recipe cost editor tab. Keep any unsaved edits in that tab. Current BOBS standards come from the separately saved market references and BOBS calibration; this historical list is not the operational fallback.

To correct a shared BOBS standard: the person maintaining BOBS must compare the proposed change with the current saved standard, back it up, and save the approved revision through a separate process.

How the historical costs are shown

ingredients + fuel + yield

Batch COGS = food/raw-material inputs + directly attributable batch energy. Unit COGS = batch COGS ÷ standard yield. Labour remains outside Actual COGS and is handled by Workload/Staffing and outlet economics.

These totals use the rates saved in the historical record. They are not current purchase prices and cannot be edited here.
