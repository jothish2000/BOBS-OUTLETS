Supply & quantity

Sold Today counts separately sold packs only. Complimentary portions with another product are costed on that product, not entered here. Sambar starts at 200 ml, using the original Recipe Master.

Quantity today = available sales units per batch × batches. For Sides & Extras, enter packs allocated to separate sales, not the whole shared kitchen batch. Purchase costing details describe a supplier unit or batch; they do not record another stock receipt.

Prefilled catalogue prices are unverified planning references, not current industry-standard prices. Replace them with your actual invoice and selling prices.

02 · Complimentary condiments & poriyal

These portions are included with this product: cost is counted, but there is no separate selling price. Choose Production or Purchase independently for every side. Extra paid packets are entered separately under Sides & Extras.

Poriyal: start with 50 g per rice plate/packet (editable planning allowance). This is not a universal industry standard. Check your actual serving and cooked batch yield. Recipes need your current rates before their cost is complete.

03 · Consolidated COGS & pricing

Each component includes its own packing. Expand a row to see food and packing separately. Packing is not added again to the overall total.

Existing saved items retain their previous UUWP rule; change it only if intended. UUWP is a pricing allowance, not another cash expense. Enter 0 where an allowance is not needed. Avoid adding spoilage again if the recipe's final yield already includes that loss. Markup is not profit margin.
