02 · Complimentary condiments & poriyal

These portions are included with this product: cost is counted, but there is no separate selling price. Choose Production or Purchase independently for every side. Extra paid packets are entered separately under Sides & Extras.

Poriyal: start with 50 g per rice plate/packet (editable planning allowance). This is not a universal industry standard. Check your actual serving and cooked batch yield. Recipes need your current rates before their cost is complete.
