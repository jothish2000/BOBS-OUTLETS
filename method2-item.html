01 · Supply & quantity

Quantity today = batch quantity × batches. Leftovers are shown separately, not silently added to sold quantity. A prefilled supplier rate is an old catalogue estimate; replace it with your invoice rate.

02 · Condiments & poriyal

Choose only what you serve with this item. Each selection gets its own recipe link. Purchased sides use their own supplier rates.

Poriyal: start with 50 g per rice plate/packet (editable planning allowance). This is not a universal industry standard. Check your actual serving and cooked batch yield. Recipes need your current rates before their cost is complete.

03 · One serving's packing → cost per item

Enter the empty containers used together for ONE packed serving. The food inside is already costed under Condiments; do not enter the sambar food price here.

Each row: containers used × price each ÷ items sharing the pack. Sold Today is rounded up to whole packs; a partially filled final pack still incurs its full packing cost.

04 · COGS & pricing

UUWP is a pricing allowance, not an additional cash purchase. It applies to resale; for production it applies when there are no known leftovers. Markup is not profit margin.
