How staff cost works

Each staff member has a Home Outlet for normal cost ownership. The roster records where the person actually works today. The report shows both Home Staff Cost and Today's Deployed Staff Cost. This lets you see a normal 6-staff RSP / 4-staff GP plan, while also seeing a rescue move such as GP staff temporarily working at RSP.
