How BOBS calculates it

1. Monthly employee CTC is allocated to the outlet and converted to daily cost using 30 days/month. 2. Outlet rent, EB and other monthly costs are converted to daily cost. 3. Daily COGS is taken from the selected Method 1/2 sales model. 4. Break-even Sales = Daily operating cost ÷ Gross Margin %. This is a financial operating target, not a roster instruction.
